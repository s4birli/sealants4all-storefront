## Faulty or damaged products

In the unlikely event that a delivered product is faulty or damaged, we will exchange the item or provide a full refund if you notify us within **14 days** in writing to [info@sealants4all.co.uk](mailto:info@sealants4all.co.uk). Please include detailed information and images where possible, along with your order / sales / invoice number.

We reserve the right to inspect all returned products before agreeing to any refund or exchange. Once notified, we arrange collection at our expense and require the return within 14 days, after which we will dispatch a replacement or process the refund to the original payment account.

We are not liable for incidental work or expenses arising from product defects or poor workmanship in application. Liability for manufacturer defects is limited to replacement of the faulty goods only, and applies to the specific defective goods rather than the entire order.

## Returns and refunds

Standard products may be returned within **14 days** of the transport date. Refunds are provided minus a **30% restocking fee**. Goods must arrive in original, undamaged, unused condition in resaleable packaging. You bear the return shipping costs.

We retain the right to refuse refunds on unsaleable items and assume no responsibility for items lost or damaged during return transit. Refunds are issued within 14 days of inspection.

Custom-made products — such as glass or wood doors, sliding systems, frames or other customised products — cannot be returned.

## Cancellation

You may cancel orders for standard products up to **14 days** after delivery by emailing [info@sealants4all.co.uk](mailto:info@sealants4all.co.uk). Cancellation rights do not apply to custom-made or bespoke items. Return shipping costs are your responsibility.
