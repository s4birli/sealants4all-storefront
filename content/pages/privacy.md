## Important information and who we are

This privacy policy explains how Sealants4All collects and processes personal data through use of our website, newsletter signups, and product purchases. The website is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect data relating to minors.

**Controller:** ALL 4 CONSTRUCTION LIMITED T/A SEALANTS4ALL is the controller of your personal data.

**Contact:** [support@sealants4all.co.uk](mailto:support@sealants4all.co.uk)

The website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies independently.

## The data we collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

- **Identity Data:** name, username, marital status, date of birth, gender
- **Contact Data:** addresses, email, phone numbers
- **Financial Data:** bank and payment card details
- **Transaction Data:** payment details and purchase history
- **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, location
- **Profile Data:** username, password, purchase history, preferences
- **Usage Data:** how you interact with the website
- **Marketing and Communications Data:** your preferences

We do not collect Special Categories of Personal Data (such as race, religion, sexual orientation, health, or genetic data) or criminal conviction data.

Data is obtained through direct interactions (forms, calls, emails), automated technologies (cookies and similar tools), and third parties (analytics providers, payment processors, and public sources).

## How we use your data

We process personal data when performing a contract with you, where it is in our legitimate interests, where required by law, or where you have provided consent (primarily for third-party marketing). Purposes include account management, order processing and payment, relationship management, business administration and security, analytics and service improvement, and personalised product recommendations.

## Marketing

We send marketing only where you have requested information or purchased from us and have not opted out. Express opt-in consent is required before sharing your data with third parties for marketing. You can adjust your preferences through your account settings at any time.

## Cookies

You can set your browser to refuse cookies, though this may limit website functionality. You can also manage your consent at any time using the **Consent Preferences** control in our website footer.

## Data sharing

We may share personal data with our internal departments, external service providers, professional advisers, regulatory authorities, and potential business acquirers. All third parties are required to respect the security of your data and comply with the law.

## Data security and retention

We have appropriate safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, loss or disclosure, and access is limited to those with a legitimate business need. Basic customer information is retained for six years after the customer relationship ends for tax purposes, after which data may be anonymised.

## Your legal rights

You have the right to request access to, correction or erasure of your personal data; to object to or restrict processing; to request data transfer; and to withdraw consent. There is usually no fee, and we aim to respond within one month. We may request information to verify your identity before responding.
