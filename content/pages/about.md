## A trade partner, not just a supplier

All 4 Construction Limited is a prominent supplier of superior building materials to the UK construction sector, with **over two decades** of experience built on excellence and reliability. We see ourselves as an active collaborator in your projects rather than simply a vendor.

**Sealants4All** is a key division of the All 4 Construction group, broadening our material offering with a focused range of trade-grade sealants, adhesives, fixings and external wall insulation (EWI) systems.

## Why customers choose us

- **Official brands.** We supply genuine products from [Sika](/search?q=Sika), [Fischer](/search?q=Fischer), [Soudal](/search?q=Soudal), [Teroson](/search?q=Teroson), [Terraco](/search?q=Terraco) and Everbuild.
- **24-hour dispatch.** Most in-stock orders leave our warehouse within 24 hours.
- **Trade pricing.** Volume pricing and trade accounts for professionals — [open a trade account](/trade).
- **Technical know-how.** Our team helps you specify the right product for the job, from joint sealing to chemical anchoring.

We understand the unique demands of both small-scale jobs and large developments. Our advantage comes from a comprehensive product range combined with a team committed to your success.
