All deliveries are made from our warehouse to your address by carrier, bundled and boxed, to the kerbside. For custom or bulk pricing, please [contact us](/contact). Most in-stock orders are dispatched within 24 hours.

## Inspecting and signing for goods

You must inspect all goods on arrival. If items arrive damaged, **refuse the delivery**. All goods need to be signed for, so please supply an address where someone will be in to receive the delivery. Inspect goods before signing and reject damaged items — please do not sign and later claim damage, as you would then cover the return costs.

## Failed delivery attempts

If nobody is present, the carrier attempts delivery twice and leaves a card. After two failed attempts, goods are returned to the local depot, where you may collect them or arrange re-delivery (charged). If you decline both options, you are responsible for the two-way charge (the delivery attempt plus return to depot).

## Changing your delivery address

Goods are shipped to the address on your order. You can change the address at the time of ordering at no extra cost. However, if you change your delivery address **after** your goods have been dispatched, an extra delivery charge will apply.

## Returns

| Reason | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Ordered in error / wrong size | Return within 14 days with a 30% restocking fee. Request a return form and use a reputable carrier. |
| Custom-made items | Cannot be returned. |
| Faulty items | We arrange collection and replace or refund the item plus carriage costs. |
| Incorrectly supplied items | We arrange collection, send a replacement, and process your return. |

See our full [Refund & Returns Policy](/returns) for details.
