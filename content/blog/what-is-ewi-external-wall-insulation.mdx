**External Wall Insulation (EWI)** wraps a building's outside walls in a layer of insulation, then protects it with a reinforced render. It cuts heat loss, eliminates cold spots, and gives tired façades a clean modern finish — all without losing internal floor space. Here's how a complete EWI system is built up, layer by layer.

## Why EWI instead of internal insulation?

- **No lost room space** — the insulation goes on the outside.
- **No thermal bridging** — the wrap is continuous, so cold spots and condensation risk drop.
- **Thermal mass stays warm** — the wall sits inside the insulation, evening out temperature swings.
- **Façade refresh** — the render finish renews the look of dated brick or pebbledash.

It's one of the most effective upgrades for solid-wall properties that can't take cavity insulation.

## The EWI build-up, layer by layer

An EWI system is exactly that — a **system**. Each layer is designed to work with the next, and mixing incompatible products is the fastest route to cracking and de-bonding. From the wall outward:

1. **Adhesive / basecoat** — bonds the insulation board to the substrate. Often the same polymer-modified mortar used later for the basecoat.
2. **Insulation board** — typically EPS, mineral wool or phenolic. Thickness is set by the U-value target.
3. **Mechanical fixings** — insulation [fixings and anchors](/category/fixing) pin the boards back to the wall as a belt-and-braces fix alongside the adhesive.
4. **Reinforced basecoat + mesh** — a layer of basecoat with an embedded fibreglass **reinforcing mesh** that controls cracking and gives the system its impact resistance.
5. **Primer** — a [primer](/category/primers) keys the basecoat for the topcoat and regulates suction for an even finish.
6. **Decorative render** — the weatherproof, UV-stable topcoat: silicone, silicone-acrylic, mineral or textured. This is the bit you see.

Skip or substitute a layer and the whole wall is compromised — the system is only certified as a complete kit.

## Choosing your materials

- **Adhesive & basecoat mortar** — polymer-modified for flexibility and grab.
- **Insulation** — EPS for value, mineral wool for fire performance and breathability, phenolic for the thinnest build-up.
- **Mesh** — alkali-resistant fibreglass, fully embedded (never dry-fixed).
- **Render** — silicone renders for self-cleaning and breathability; textured or [decorative renders](/category/ewi) for the finish you want.

## Common EWI mistakes to avoid

- **Mixing brands across layers** — stick to one certified system.
- **Skipping mechanical fixings** — adhesive alone isn't enough on most substrates.
- **Under-embedding the mesh** — it must sit in the outer third of the basecoat, fully covered.
- **Rendering in the wrong weather** — too hot, too cold or too wet ruins the cure.
- **No primer before the topcoat** — uneven suction shows up as patchy render.
